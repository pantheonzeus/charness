# Category: strategy

**Scope:** Strategy-consulting analytical frameworks — diagnosis, market/competitive intelligence, strategic choice and economics, operating-model execution, risk/value governance, and executive alignment. Generic engagement-method skills, not tied to a specific client or vault entity (contrast with `sales`, which is deal/pursuit-specific).

**What belongs here:** situation and growth diagnosis, market sizing and segmentation, competitor-move modelling, options/pricing/portfolio/business-case economics, operating-model and roadmap design, KPI/risk/value governance, and stakeholder/narrative/decision-memo communication.

**Sub-domains (conceptual grouping, not physical subfolders — flat until the category exceeds ~200 skills):**
1. **Diagnosis and framing** — `situation-assessment`, `growth-barriers`, `assumption-audit`, `venture-idea-teardown` (house-authored, not part of the import)
2. **Market and competitive intelligence** — `market-mapping`, `competitive-intel`, `customer-segmentation`, `profit-pool-analysis`
3. **Strategic choice and economics** — `strategic-options`, `pricing-strategy`, `business-case-builder`, `portfolio-review`
4. **Operating model and execution** — `operating-model-design`, `initiative-prioritizer`, `transformation-roadmap`
5. **Risk, performance, and value governance** — `war-gaming`, `risk-and-mitigation`, `kpi-architect`, `value-realization`
6. **Alignment and executive communication** — `stakeholder-alignment`, `narrative-builder`, `decision-memo`

# LSC 24 — Trends (Management Track)

## Contract
- **Input:** the business domain and client segment; optionally the draft objective from `lsc-strategic-business-objectives` (25) or 01.
- **Output:** `canvases/24-trends.md`: chosen megatrends, evidence of each in news and in culture/products, and a ranked opportunity and threat list for this project.
- **Allowed tools/artifacts:** web search, news and trade-press scans, `agent-augmented-discovery` for a research swarm, management and team input.
- **Done when:** each chosen trend has dated, cited examples from both sources of signal, and the final list separates opportunities from threats with the decision each one affects.

## Source and scope
From the Lean Service Creation Handbook 2.0 (Futurice, 2018), Management Track. It is not in the 23-page toolbox PDF. The book's framing: form strategy from trends rather than reacting to competitors; give it as homework to team and management so it fills over days. Canvas fields: **Megatrends** · **How does the megatrend show up in the daily news** · **How does the megatrend show up in advertisement, media, social media, entertainment and products** · **Trends and phenomena that influence this project**.

## Core Blueprint
1. **Choose megatrends.** Pick the few that matter to the business and its clients; add domain-specific ones. Write each as a statement of change, not a topic ("operators demand data sovereignty", not "data").
2. **News signal.** For each trend find recent articles in the domain and in general press. Record headline, source, date, and the tone (hype, worry, regulation, resignation). Tone shows how far the trend has travelled.
3. **Culture and product signal.** Do the same for advertising, media, social media, entertainment and shipped products. This shows value shifts and what mainstream customers are being taught to expect. **[house]** Cite the concrete product or campaign.
4. **Analyse.** For each trend: opportunity or threat for this project, the customer need it touches (link to 02/05 later), time horizon, and the decision it changes (scope, timing, partner, price).
5. **Rank** by impact and certainty. Mark which are `[O]` (cited developments) and which are `[I]` (our extrapolation).
6. **Feed forward.** Opportunities become candidates for the objective in 25 or 01 and for the Back-to-the-future field in `lsc-feasibility-study` (13). Threats become risks and kill triggers in 01.

## Quality tests
- Every trend is a change statement with at least two dated, cited examples from different sources.
- Both signal columns filled.
- Each opportunity/threat names the decision it affects.
- Forecast statements are tagged `[I]`; no invented statistics.

## Agent-augmented variant
A research swarm can sweep news and product launches per trend as file-backed children (Charter §5), returning sourced findings. The parent spot-checks sources and prefers primary sources and trade press over content farms (Charter §2). Agent summaries are `[S]` until the source is read.

## Gotchas
- **Trend tourism.** A list of buzzwords with no consequence for a decision is decoration.
- **Only industry press.** Value shifts show first outside the industry, in media and consumer products.
- **Treating hype as demand.** Tone in news is not customer behaviour; test in 15 and 21.

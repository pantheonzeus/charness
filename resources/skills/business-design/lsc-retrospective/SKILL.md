# LSC 22 — Retrospective

## Contract
- **Input:** previous retro file, `canvases/19-weekly-board.md`, `canvases/20-agile-board.md`, team observations.
- **Output:** `canvases/22-retrospective.md`, one dated section per retro with the lists, the 1–4 chosen changes (owner, date), and the review of the last retro's changes.
- **Allowed tools/artifacts:** the team in a room or call; an agent as a scribe and clustering aid.
- **Done when:** the last retro's changes are reviewed first (done or not, effect), the three lists are filled, one to four changes are chosen with owners and review date, and any change that worked is sent to the Retro Diamond.

## Canvas (v3.0.5) — "Reflect, learn, change, and share"
**Keep doing:** *What's going well? What should we keep doing?* (the same canvas can serve multiple retros) · **Do more/start:** *What should the team do more or start doing?* · **Do less/stop:** *What should the team do less or stop altogether?* · **TO DO:** *Select (1-4) which items to change next. Who is responsible?* · **DONE:** *Start the next retro by going through the results of the last retro.* · *What change did you make based on the last retro that worked well? Add it to the Retro Diamond canvas and remember to share with other teams as well!*

## Core Blueprint
1. **Open with DONE.** Read the last retro's chosen changes: done, not done, effect observed. Anything not done gets a reason, not a pass.
2. **Gather individually, silently** (5 minutes) into the three lists, then cluster. Everyone writes; the loud voices do not set the list.
3. **Discuss the biggest clusters,** time-boxed. Distinguish things we control from things we escalate.
4. **Choose 1–4 changes,** no more. Each change: a concrete action, an owner, a date to check, and the signal that will show it worked. **[house]**
5. **Record to TO DO.** Mirror the actions in 20 so they are visible work.
6. **Close:** appreciation round; what to carry to the next retro.
7. **After the retro:** changes that worked in a later review go to 23; patterns that repeat go to the sponsor as an organisational issue.

## Quality tests
- DONE reviewed before new items.
- Three lists have entries from at least most team members (check participation).
- 1–4 changes; each has owner, date, success signal.
- Changes are actions, not wishes ("write a definition of ready by Friday", not "communicate better").

## Agent-augmented variant
An agent may cluster notes, count themes across retros and flag recurring issues (`[S]`). It may not speak for the team or attribute statements to people. Psychological safety first: the team decides what gets recorded.

## Handbook 2.0 guidance (book, Futurice 2018, paraphrased)
Handbook guidance:
- Change the retro format from time to time. A retro should be short once the team knows the drill. Take a few minutes in silence before sharing.
- Keep doing: state the obvious things that work, not only what to improve.
- To do: vote on one to four changes and owners. Do not choose too many topics. Add them to the agile board.
- Done: the next retro starts by reviewing the last one's items. Pick one change that really worked and add it to the Retro Diamond.

## Gotchas
- **Action overload.** Four is the limit on the canvas; more means none.
- **Blame retros.** Focus on the system and the next change.
- **Retro without follow-up.** The next retro starts with DONE for this reason.

## Hand-offs
Worked changes → `lsc-retro-diamond` (23). Board updates → `lsc-agile-board` (20).

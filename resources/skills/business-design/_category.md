# Category: business-design

**Scope:** The business designer's method: the **viability** owner in a multidisciplinary design team, sitting between strategy, design and technology. It covers framing opportunities, designing value propositions and business models (including as-a-service, platform and AI-native models), proving the money at concept stage, and testing the riskiest assumptions with experiments. It includes both classic practice and agent-augmented practice. Generic method, not tied to a client (contrast with `sales`).

**Boundaries:**
- `strategy/` owns board-grade analysis the business designer hands off to: `business-case-builder` (NPV/financing), `pricing-strategy` (price levels), `assumption-audit` (assumption register), `market-mapping`, `customer-segmentation`, `kpi-architect`, `narrative-builder`, `operating-model-design`.
- `service-design/` owns the customer and service-delivery side: real-user research, journeys, blueprints, usability/operability prototyping, co-design facilitation, per-service agentic blueprints, services for customer-side agents. This category owns the business model, ecosystem strategy, servitization typology, AI-native models, and viability experiments.
- `technology/cloud-architecture` owns the technical architecture behind connected-asset and AI offers.

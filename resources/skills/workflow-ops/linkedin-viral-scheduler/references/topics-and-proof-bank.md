## Confidentiality boundary (non-negotiable — Soul.md inviolable line)
Every proof point above is already public (LinkedIn profile / public consortium record). Do not introduce non-public client specifics, financial figures beyond what's already public (60% overhead cut, 19%+ margins — both already on the public profile), or any detail from a current or former employer's client engagement not named above. When in doubt, abstract to the pattern, not the instance.

## Recency-guard convention
`output/linkedin/history.tsv` — one row per finalized post: `date  joint  dialect  topic  file` (columns extended 2026-08-22). **The guard rotates `joint`**: before picking, exclude a joint used in the last 2 runs unless every alternative scores materially worse on story availability. A topic may legitimately recur across different joints — the same servitisation evidence reads differently at J1 than at J3 — so the topic column is recorded but no longer gates.

## Story-first bias (added 2026-07-19)
Every topic above must be **entered through a lived moment** (a meeting, a decision, a setback, a real conversation) and **framed for a manufacturing/industrial buyer**, never pitched as a capability announcement — see `engagement-learnings.md` for the evidence. A topic pick with a strong proof point but no available moment behind it should be deprioritized in favor of one that has both.

**Hardened 2026-08-22:** if *no* candidate has a genuine moment, the correct output is **"no post this run"**, not a draft from a clean angle. Five posts in August 2026 were abandoned mid-iteration for exactly this reason — the engine outran its material. Moment supply is now a scheduled activity (plant visits, customer conversations, consortium travel) rather than something assumed to exist.

## Methodology guard (added 2026-08-22)
The joints are an **internal** diagnostic. Never present the ladder in public as a named model — the sequence is published prior art (see `ladder-joints.md` §0), and a signature framework is the artifact class that lost the Company A hearing to a panel explicitly testing *"Umsetzer oder Berater?"*. Lead with the stall, not the stages.
